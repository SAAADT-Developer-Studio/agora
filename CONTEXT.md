# Vidik

News articles grouped by the same real-world happening.

## Language

**Story**:
Articles covering the same news event, with membership that grows as later articles join.
_Avoid_: Cluster, topic, event, thread

**Cluster**:
Articles covering the same news event, grouped as a batch snapshot.
_Avoid_: Story

**Membership**:
The link of one article to exactly one Story.
_Avoid_: Article-cluster, label

**Assignment**:
The decision that an article joins an existing Story or seeds a new one.
_Avoid_: Classification, cluster label
